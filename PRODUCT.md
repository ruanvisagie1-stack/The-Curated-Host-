## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js + Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui (user-specified in the original request).

## Users

Two primary audiences, served by the same site:

1. **Travelers** researching or ready to book one of Ruan's own Airbnb apartments — they browse listings on the site, then click through to the live Airbnb listing to book, or message his business WhatsApp to ask about availability directly.
2. **Property owners** (first-time or seasoned hosts) considering hiring Ruan for boutique Airbnb co-hosting / short-term rental management — they read his positioning and services, then request a free consultation or property audit.

## Product Purpose

Showcase Ruan Cloete's Airbnb apartment listings to drive direct bookings/inquiries, and present his co-hosting & rental-management service to win new homeowner clients. Two calls to action: "View on Airbnb" / "Enquire on WhatsApp" for guests, and "Request a consultation/property audit" for owners.

## Positioning

Boutique, quality-over-quantity co-hosting — a limited number of properties, each treated with owner-level care, versus mass-market Airbnb management companies. Backed by 5+ years managing group accommodations, 80+ long-term rentals managed in Johannesburg, and personal operation of his own successful Airbnb in Green Point, Cape Town.

## Product Principles

1. Guest-facing content must always resolve to a real action: view the live Airbnb listing or open WhatsApp — never a dead end.
2. Owner-facing (co-hosting) content sells trust and personal care, not scale — avoid mass-market management company tropes.
3. Never invent testimonials, guest counts, review scores, or specific property details beyond what's supplied.
4. Placeholder imagery/palette must be easy to swap wholesale once real assets arrive — don't hardcode placeholder choices deep into markup.
